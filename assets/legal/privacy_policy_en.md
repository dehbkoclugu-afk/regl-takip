# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 26, 2026**

At CEY Studio, we respect your privacy while you use the "Cycle Tracker - Period Calendar" app. This privacy policy explains how our app handles your data.

## 1. Data We Collect

Our app stores the following information locally on your device only:

- User name and date of birth
- Menstrual cycle data (start/end dates, cycle length)
- Daily health records (mood, symptoms, flow intensity)
- Water intake, sleep, weight, and body temperature data
- Medication reminder details
- Sexual activity records
- Notes

## 2. Data Storage

All of your personal data is stored only on your device, encrypted. Your data is never sent to our or anyone else's servers or cloud services. The only exception is the Health integration, which runs only when you start it (see Section 4). Period tracking works without an internet connection; purchases, ads and optional crash reporting require internet access.

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with any third party. The app does not sell, rent, or commercially exploit your personal data. Your health data is never used for advertising or marketing.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Your health data is never sent to any third party unless you use the Health integration below yourself. The third-party services used by the app are:

- **Health Connect (Android) / Apple Health (iOS)**: Used only when you start it from settings and grant permission. Writing: your period days are exported to Health Connect / the Health app on your device; exported days of records you later delete or shorten in the app are removed on the next sync. Reading: period days from the last 12 months are shown to you for confirmation before being added to the app. This data is used only for period tracking, is never used for advertising or any other purpose, and is not transferred to third parties. You can revoke these permissions at any time in Health Connect / Health settings.

- **Google Play Billing**: Used for premium purchases. These transactions are subject to Google's own privacy policy.
- **Google AdMob**: Used to show ads in the free version. AdMob may process technical data such as device identifiers to serve ads; it cannot access your health data. Users in the EU/UK are separately asked for consent for ad personalization. The premium version contains no ads and no AdMob.
- **Crash reporting (Sentry)**: Only in builds where it is enabled, technical error information is sent when the app crashes. Sending of personal and health data is disabled.

## 5. Notifications

The app uses your device's notification system to deliver reminders. These notifications are entirely local and do not send data to any server.

## 6. Security

The app can be protected with a PIN code and biometric authentication (fingerprint/face recognition). This security information is stored encrypted on your device.

## 7. Data Export and Deletion

- You can export your data at any time in CSV or PDF format, or create a password-encrypted backup. Temporary export files are removed from the device the next time the app starts.
- You can permanently delete all of your data with "Delete all data" in settings or by removing the app. Data previously exported to Health Connect / Health stays in those apps; you can delete it there separately.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page.
